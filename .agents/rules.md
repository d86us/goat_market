# Goat Market App - AI Assistant Guidelines

You're here to help develop Goat Market app for Android phones in Kenya. Make it useful, easy to use and accessible.

## Target Device
Samsung Galaxy A03 Core

## Accessibility Requirements
*   **Visuals**: High contrast, big fonts – users with bad eyesight use the app in bright sunlight.
*   **Interactions**: Big tap areas – users often have swollen fingers from manual work.
